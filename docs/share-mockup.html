Share, dark

A dark take on your orientation mockups, stretched over the whole system. One Go binary serves the website and the API and keeps every file in a folder set in config.json, on whatever machine runs it: a small computer at home, a VPS or a container. The website is for sending: anyone with a PIN, no account. People with an account can do everything there that the app does (22–37). Nothing is family-specific; friends and anyone else with a PIN or an invite see the same screens. The app is for seeing and downloading, and for admins, for running the whole thing. Numbers follow the order in which you'd meet the screens.

Anyone with a PIN can send

Uppy runs headless underneath: tus for resumable uploads, Golden Retriever to survive a closed tab. The screens on top are our own, because Uppy's stock Dashboard shows far more than someone sending a few photos needs. Tablets and computers get their own layouts, below. The language comes from the browser; the globe button on the first screens switches it. A tunnel or a reverse proxy in front provides the HTTPS a PWA needs.

Proposal · ZIP

Full quality over WhatsApp, as a ZIP

WhatsApp makes photos smaller and videos much smaller, unless they come as a document. So Share packs them into a ZIP, which WhatsApp passes on byte for byte, and Share on the other phone saves what's inside into the gallery. Making and opening a ZIP happens on the phones alone, with no server, no account and no internet. A ZIP may only be as big as the app that carries it takes, 2 GB for WhatsApp, so Share asks where it goes and makes as many as needed. Each one opens on its own; only a file bigger than one ZIP is cut into pieces, and Share on the other phone puts them back together. Every ZIP also holds share.json, a short list of what's in it and how the parts belong together. Without Share, any phone or computer unpacks the ZIPs, and 7-Zip joins a cut video's pieces. Only in the app, for now.
